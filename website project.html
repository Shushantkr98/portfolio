About

Amrit Udyan

Spread over a vast expanse of 15 acres, Amrit Udyan has often been portrayed, and deservedly so, as the soul of the Rashtrapati Bhavan. Originally, it included East Lawn, Central Lawn, Long Garden and Circular Garden. During the term of former Presidents Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam and Shri Ram Nath Kovind, more gardens were developed, namely, Herbal, Tactile Garden, Bonsai Garden and Arogya Vanam.

This edition of Amrit Udyan will be a landscaping marvel where visitors can witness rare seasonal flowers in full glory. This season Amrit Udyan introduces a new highlight — the Babbling Brook, featuring a meandering stream, sculptural spouts, stepping stones, and a reflecting pool, offering a refreshing and serene experience.

Further, visitors can spend time in multiple attractions, a specially curated garden for children called Bal Vatika with a story of a 225-year-old Sheesham tree, a treehouse, Nature’s classroom etc. Then there are the Bonsai, Circular Gardens with a diverse variety of flora and fauna.
